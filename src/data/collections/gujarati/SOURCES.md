# Gujarati collection research and image notes

All eight entries in `sarees.ts` are DRIFT concept studies inspired by Gujarati craft references, not seller-verified products. They use Price on request, zero ratings/reviews/stock, and unavailable status. Materials, zari, dimensions, care, maker, production origin/provenance, and exact SKU details are explicitly pending confirmation. Craft references are design inspiration, not claims about the production of a purchasable item.

Craft references checked (14 Nov 2026):

- Intellectual Property India GI search, Patan Double Ikat Patola application: https://search.ipindia.gov.in/GIRPublicSearch/Application/Details/232
- Ministry of Textiles / Handicrafts GI compendium, Patola described as a silk double ikat from Patan, Gujarat: https://www.handicrafts.nic.in/CmsUpload/12222017102212GI%20BOOK%20FINAL%202-5-17_resized.pdf
- Ministry of Culture, Incredible India, Patan Patola overview: https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/gujarat/patan-patola
- Asia InCH, Gujarat Gharchola craft: https://asiainch.org/craft/gharchola-craft-of-gujarat/
- Gaatha, Kutch Bandhani documentation: https://gaatha.org/Craft-of-India/research-kutch-bandhani-textile/
- Indian Express reported Patan Patola saree prices from roughly ₹1.5 lakh to ₹6 lakh and described the time-intensive double-ikat process: https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/modi-gifts-italian-pm-a-patan-patola-scarf-what-is-the-ancient-gujarat-art-form-is-8272441/lite/

## Asset audit

Existing files under `public/collections/gujarati/` are present and decode as JPEGs, but are not a verified product-image set. Hash inspection found only a handful of unique images repeatedly assigned to the primary/detail/editorial/pallu slots; at least one inspected image is a generic purple brocade saree and cannot substantiate a Patola, Bandhani, Panetar, Gharchola, or Ashavali SKU. Their original source and license are not documented. Keep them as temporary generic editorial placeholders only, do not label them as exact product views, and replace them with brand-owned or explicitly licensed assets before launch.

Potential reference image sources (not downloaded because their licensing/usage fit has not been cleared for use as commercial product photography): Wikimedia Commons `Category:Patola Sari` and `Category:Bandhani saris`. Museum textiles are useful craft references but are not photos of these fictional SKUs.
