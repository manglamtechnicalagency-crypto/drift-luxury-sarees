# Heritage-state collection research and data limits

Checked 15 November 2026. The eight `sarees.ts` entries are DRIFT concept studies, not sourced seller SKUs. They intentionally have no sale price, availability, stock, ratings, review counts, maker attribution, exact fibre composition, measured dimensions, or confirmed GI provenance. “Price on request” means no offer is currently available. Market prices below are context from other products and must not be copied onto these concepts.

## Craft and geographic references

- Government of India, Development Commissioner (Handlooms), *Study and Documentation of Paithani Sarees & Dress Materials*: describes Paithani as a handwoven silk sari with ornamental zari pallu and border, and documents the craft and clusters: https://handlooms.gov.in/assets/img/Publications/Paithani%20sarees%20and%20Dress%20Materials635701517283000941.pdf
- Intellectual Property India, registered GI application 150, “Paithani Saree & Fabrics”: https://search.ipindia.gov.in/GIRPublicSearch/Application/Details/150
- Press Information Bureau, Government of India, 2025 announcement on Paithani revival and market context; reports a broad market range from ₹15,000 to over ₹5 lakh, not a quote for these designs: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2174967&lang=2&reg=48
- Intellectual Property India, “Chanderi Sarees” GI record: https://search.ipindia.gov.in/GIRPublicSearch/Application/Details/7
- Development Commissioner (Handlooms) weaver profile PDF: describes Chanderi as silk warp with mercerised cotton weft and a Nakshi Meenakari border; this describes the craft/profile item, not any DRIFT SKU: https://www.handlooms.gov.in/assets/img/Weavers%20Database/MP637322653330389383.pdf
- Intellectual Property India, registered handloom GI list, records Sambalpuri Bandha Saree & Fabrics: https://handlooms.gov.in/assets/img/information_handlooms/Details%20of%20102GI%20registered%20Handloom%20Products%20under%20GI%20ACT%2C%201999%20%28as%20on%2015.03.2023_removed.pdf
- Utkalika, Odisha State Co-operative Handicrafts Corporation Ltd. (state handloom corporation), Sambalpuri silk listings showed product-specific list prices around ₹22,661–₹37,632 and sale prices around ₹16,708–₹30,106 when checked. Listings vary by SKU and stock status; this is only a market snapshot: https://utkalikaodisha.com/product-category/handloom-of-odisha/silksaree/
- Ministry of Textiles, *Sustainability in the Handloom Traditions of India*: documents Muga silk's Assam GI context, use in Assamese garments including mekhela chador and sarees, and a ₹35,000–₹1,50,000 range for a Muga mekhela chador in the publication. This is not a current Muga saree quote: https://www.handlooms.gov.in/assets/img/EBOOK/Sustainability%20in%20the%20Handloom%20Traditions%20of%20India.pdf
- Assam Loom House catalogue (seller catalogue for Assam silks; check live SKU-level prices and GI evidence directly before relying on it): https://assamloomhouse.com/

## Asset audit

All 34 existing JPG files under `public/collections/heritage-states/` are present, but they are not a verified product-image set. SHA-256 inspection found repeated photographs across multiple primary/detail/editorial/pallu slots; the original image source and reuse license are not recorded. They must be treated as generic editorial placeholders, not proof of a named weave, material, origin, or SKU. I did not download/rehost seller images because commercial product-image reuse permission was not verified. Replace these files with brand-owned or explicitly licensed photography before presenting these concept studies as purchasable products.
