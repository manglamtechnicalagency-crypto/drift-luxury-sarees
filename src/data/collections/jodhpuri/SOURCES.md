## Craft and region references

- Government of Rajasthan, Handmade in Rajasthan, “Bandhej and Leheriya”: documents the tie-and-dye process and names Bandhni, Lehriya, Mothda, Ekdali, and Shikari pattern methods: https://handmadeinrajasthan.rajasthan.gov.in/content/industries/handmadeinrajasthandepartment/artandcraft/textilework/tyeanddyetobereplacedwithbandhejandlehriya.html
- Intellectual Property India, registered GI detail for Jodhpur Bandhej Craft (application 751): https://search.ipindia.gov.in/GIRPublicSearch/Application/Details/751
- Rajasthan Tourism, “Shopping in Rajasthan”: identifies Jodhpur, Jaipur, and Bikaner as Bandhej tie-dye centres and mentions Leheriya sarees: https://www.tourism.rajasthan.gov.in/content/rajasthan-tourism/en/shopping-in-rajasthan.html
- Rana's by Kshitija, pure Leheriya collection: seller states handcrafted examples range from about ₹6,000 to over ₹80,000 depending on textile and embellishment; use as a broad market context only: https://ranasaree.com/collections/pure-leheriya-sarees
