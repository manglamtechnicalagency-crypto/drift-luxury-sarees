## Craft and region references

- [Government of Rajasthan, Handmade in Rajasthan — Bandhej and Leheriya](https://handmadeinrajasthan.rajasthan.gov.in/content/industries/handmadeinrajasthandepartment/artandcraft/textilework/tyeanddyetobereplacedwithbandhejandlehriya.html): documents resist-dye processes and names Bandhej, Leheriya, Mothda, Ekdali, and Shikari methods.
- [Intellectual Property India — Jodhpur Bandhej Craft GI record](https://search.ipindia.gov.in/GIRPublicSearch/Application/Details/751).
- [Rajasthan Tourism — Shopping in Rajasthan](https://www.tourism.rajasthan.gov.in/content/rajasthan-tourism/en/shopping-in-rajasthan.html): identifies Jodhpur, Jaipur, and Bikaner as Bandhej centres and mentions Leheriya sarees.
- [Development Commissioner (Handlooms), *The Quest for the Sacred Cloth*](https://handlooms.nic.in/assets/img/EBOOK/The_Quest_for_the_Sacred_Cloth.pdf): regional textile background.

Retail examples are not prices for these concept listings. Images are model references from Unsplash, documented in `../image-credits.md`; they do not establish craft authenticity or the pictured model’s garment provenance.
