Exact sellers, SKU pricing, material composition, embellishment, production method, lead times, availability, artisan identity, and product imagery are unverified. ## Craft facts checked

- Kota Doria is a light, open weave with small square `khat` checks, woven in the Kota region including Kaithun. Its GI registration and regional scope are recorded by [Rajasthan's Handmade in Rajasthan portal](https://handmadeinrajasthan.rajasthan.gov.in/content/industries/handmadeinrajasthandepartment/artandcraft/textilework/kotadoria.html) and [IP India's GI record](https://search.ipindia.gov.in/GIRPublicSearch/Application/Details/191).
- Leheriya and Bandhej are distinct resist-dye techniques. Jaipur, Jodhpur, and Udaipur are documented Bandhani centers; the Rajasthan portal distinguishes Leheriya from Bandhani in its [Bandhej craft page](https://handmadeinrajasthan.rajasthan.gov.in/content/industries/handmadeinrajasthandepartment/artandcraft/textilework/tyeanddyetobereplacedwithbandhejandlehriya.html).
- Bagru and Sanganeri are hand-block-print traditions associated with Rajasthan. India's Office of the Development Commissioner (Handicrafts) lists both as Rajasthan GI crafts in its [GI craft listing](https://www.handicrafts.nic.in/CmsUpload/06522024115205Approved%20Handicrafts%20Craft%20category.pdf); [Incredible India describes Sanganeri printing and its Sanganer origin](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/rajasthan/sanganeri-hand-block-printing).
