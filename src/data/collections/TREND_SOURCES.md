# DRIFT 2026/2027 Couture Trend Edit: Research & Provenance Archive

Research updated 15 November 2026 for the DRIFT Luxury Digital Flagship. `trendEdit.ts`, `colorways.ts`, and `extract-saree-trends.ts` document design research studies and conceptual visual directions across eleven distinguished Indian weaving geography clusters.

Per the **DRIFT AGENTS.md** protocol:
- All palettes, motifs, pattern sets, and pairings are cataloged explicitly as **design studies** and visual trend interpretations.
- They do not represent claims of stock inventory, commercial vendor listings, machine-replicated weaves, or verified commercial certifications.
- Every catalog entry maintains `price: 0`, `isAvailable: false`, `stockCount: 0`, `rating: 0`, and `reviewsCount: 0`.
