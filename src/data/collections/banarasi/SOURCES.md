# Banarasi / Uttar Pradesh collection notes

Checked 16 November 2026. The ten entries in `sarees.ts` are DRIFT concept studies, not verified seller listings. They use no live sale price, availability, stock, ratings, reviews, maker attribution, or confirmed material composition. Craft references describe inspiration only; they do not authenticate the pictured garments.

## Craft references

- [District Varanasi, Government of Uttar Pradesh — Banarasi Saree](https://varanasi.nic.in/district-produce/banarasi-saree/): official GI registration and craft overview.
- [Intellectual Property India — Banaras Brocades and Sarees](https://search.ipindia.gov.in/GIRPublicSearch/Application/Details/99): registered GI record.
- [Development Commissioner (Handlooms) — publications](https://handlooms.nic.in/publications.php): includes *Dream of Weaving: Study & Documentation of Banaras Sarees and Brocades* and documented varieties including Jangla and Tanchoi.

No seller-matched SKUs were found for the concepts, so prices remain on request. Current images are illustrative model portraits; they do not establish Banaras production, a particular weave, or commercial product provenance. Portrait sources are documented in `../image-credits.md`.
