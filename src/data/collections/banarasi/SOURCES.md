# Banarasi / Uttar Pradesh collection notes

This is a storefront design prototype, not a catalog of verified goods. The eight entries are craft-style studies. Their photos are generic fashion imagery and do not verify a saree, weave, material, zari, GI status, or place of origin. No artisan names, product ratings, reviews, stock counts, sale prices, or product prices are asserted. Items are marked unavailable until a seller supplies verified listing details.

## Craft references

- [District Varanasi, Government of Uttar Pradesh — Banarasi Saree](https://varanasi.nic.in/district-produce/banarasi-saree/): GI registration and official description of Banaras brocades, motifs, and handloom/jacquard practice.
- [Development Commissioner (Handlooms) — Banaras Brocades and Sarees, Chapter III](https://www.handlooms.gov.in/assets/img/Publications/Banaras%20Brocades%20and%20Sarees635701527534407243.pdf): names Jangla, Tanchoi, Vaskat, Cutwork, Tissue, and Butidar varieties and explains brocade techniques.
- [Incredible India — Banaras Brocades and Sarees](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/uttar-pradesh/banaras-brocades-and-sarees): overview of the craft and its weaving community.

## Price check and image limitations

Retail listings are not interchangeable: one Nalli Katan listing showed ₹11,062 and was sold out when checked; Sanjar Creation showed ₹19,600 for another Katan listing. These are examples only, not prices for the prototype entries. The current prototype has no seller-matched SKUs, so its prices are blank. Check current seller listings before publishing prices.

Existing local JPEGs decode, but hash inspection found that the 32 product-view slots collapse to four repeated images. These assets are not product-specific evidence. No commercial listing images were copied because reuse rights were not confirmed. Replace them with seller-provided product photos or assets with a verified reuse license before presenting the collection as merchandise.
